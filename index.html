Personal MCP Assistant

A private tool that lets its owner ask an AI assistant questions about their own email and calendar.

What it does

Personal MCP Assistant is a single-user application that runs on its owner’s own computer. It connects to the owner’s Google account through the Gmail API and the Google Calendar API, with read-only access, so the owner can search their messages and check their upcoming events by asking an AI assistant in plain language.

Who can use it

The application is not offered to the public. It has one user, the person who created it, and it does not have a server, accounts or a database.

Your data

Email and calendar data is read only when the owner asks for it, and is not stored or shared by the application. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
